Mathematical Formulation & Physics Reference

1. Spherical Earth Geometry

Given platform orbit radius $R_s = R_E + h$ and look angle $\theta_{\text{look}}$:

$$\sin(\theta_{\text{inc}}) = \frac{R_E + h}{R_E} \sin(\theta_{\text{look}})$$
$$R = \sqrt{R_E^2 \cos^2\theta_{\text{inc}} + 2 R_E h + h^2} - R_E \cos\theta_{\text{inc}}$$

Earth central angle $\beta = \theta_{\text{inc}} - \theta_{\text{look}}$, ground swath $W_g = R_E \cdot \Delta\beta$.

2. NESZ (Noise Equivalent Sigma Zero)

The radar sensitivity noise floor per unit ground area across swath:

$$\text{NESZ}(\theta) = \frac{256 \pi^3 R^3 v_s \sin(\theta_{\text{inc}}) k_B T_{\text{sys}} F_n L_{\text{tot}}}{P_{\text{avg}} G_{\text{tx}}(\theta) G_{\text{rx}}(\theta) \lambda^3 \rho_{\text{az}} \cdot \frac{c}{2 B_{\text{rf}}}}$$

Evaluates minimum detectable distributed backscatter ($\text{SNR}_{\text{dist}} = \sigma_0 / \text{NESZ}$).

3. UAV & FMCW Processing

Continuous illumination with dechirp-on-receive processing gain:

$$P_{\text{avg}} = P_t \quad (\text{Duty Cycle} = 100\%)$$
$$G_{\text{proc}} = B_{\text{rf}} \cdot T_{\text{sweep}} \cdot N_{\text{pulses}}$$

Azimuth resolution $\rho_{\text{az}} \approx L_a/2$, integration time $T_a = \frac{\lambda R}{2 \rho_{\text{az}} v}$.
